## BrewCraft Coffee-Brand Guidelines

### Brand Voice

- Warm, knowledgeable, a little playful - like a passionate barista, not a corporate marketer.
- Confident about quality, but never arrogant or dismissive of competitors.
- Avoid overly casual internet slang; avoid overly formal/corporate language.

### Tone Examples

- Do: "Slow-roasted in small batches, because good coffee can't be rushed."
- Don't: "THE BEST COFFEE ON EARTH, GUARANTEED !!! "
- Do: "A cup that pairs perfectly with a quiet morning."
- Don't: "This coffee will change your life forever."

### Prohibited Words/Phrases

- Do not use: "cure," "heal," "medically proven," "guaranteed" "#1 in the world," "best in the industry" (unverifiable superlatives).
- Do not make health claims of any kind (e.g., "boosts metabolism" "prevents illness") -BrewCraft is a food/beverage brand, not a supplement, and such claims carry legal/compliance risk.
- Avoid disparaging competitor brands by name.

### Messaging Framework

- Core message: Ethically-sourced, small-batch coffee for people who care about quality and origin.
- Target audience: Coffee enthusiasts aged 25-45, values-driven consumers who care about sourcing/sustainability.
- Key differentiators: Direct-trade relationships with tarmers, small-batch roasting, transparent sourcing (origin listed on every bag).

### Approved Claims (Verifiable)

- "Direct-trade sourced from farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala."
- "Roasted in small batches of under 50kg at a time."
- "Every bag lists the farm of origin."
